Start a jewelry business

Buy wholesale and sell your own collection

Many small jewelry businesses start by buying wholesale, adding their own style (packaging, combinations, handmade touches) and selling on social media or at local markets.

  1. Pick one niche first: for example stainless steel, macramé bracelets or personalized pieces.
  2. Order small samples before buying in quantity, and check the quality yourself.
  3. Add up the real cost: product + shipping + import fees + packaging.
  4. Take clear photos in natural light and tell the story of each piece.

Find your ring size

Know your metals

  • Stainless steel (316L): resists water and tarnish; great for everyday wear.
  • Sterling silver (925): 92.5% silver; can darken over time, polish with a soft cloth.
  • Gold-plated: a thin gold layer over another metal; keep it away from water and perfume.
  • Nickel-free: look for this label if you have sensitive skin.

Jewelry care

  • Put jewelry on last, after perfume, lotion and makeup.
  • Take it off to swim, shower, clean or exercise.
  • Store each piece separately so chains don't tangle or scratch.
  • Clean gently with a soft, dry cloth.

Two basic macramé knots

  1. Square knot: with four cords, cross the left cord over the two center cords and under the right one; bring the right cord under the center and up through the left loop. Repeat in the opposite direction.
  2. Half hitch: wrap a working cord around a holding cord and pull it through its own loop. Repeat to make a spiral.

Package forwarding

Some stores don't ship to every country. With these services you get an address in the United States: you send your order there and they forward it to you.

Shipping and import fees are paid to the forwarding company. Check their prices before you buy.
