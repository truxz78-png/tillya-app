Privacy Policy for Tillya

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This page was translated automatically. The English version is the official one.

Tillya ("the App") is a free guide to jewelry and handmade-craft stores, published by DriftforgeDev, an independent developer from the Dominican Republic. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses and why.

1. No account, no personal information collected

The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, store or sell your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The App does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

2. What the App saves on your phone

  • The language you choose in the App.
  • Translations of the App's text already made, so the App loads faster next time.
  • A copy of the App's own pages, so the App can open without internet.

This information stays on your phone and is not sent to us. You can delete it by clearing the App's data in your phone's settings.

3. Services the App uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the App's pages. Like any web server, GitHub may receive technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.
  • Google Fonts provides the App's letter styles. Google receives technical data such as your IP address when the fonts load.
  • Google Translate service: when your language is not English, the App's own text (never your personal information) is sent to Google to be translated.

Each of these companies handles that technical data under its own Privacy Policy.

4. Partner stores and affiliate links

When you tap a store button, the partner store's website opens outside the App. The store and the affiliate network that records the visit may use cookies and collect information under their own Privacy Policies. If you buy something, the store handles your order, payment and delivery data, not Tillya. The App may earn a commission on purchases made through these links, at no extra cost to you.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
